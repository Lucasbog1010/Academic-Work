# Quantum Dots: Nanostructures, Photovoltaics and Single-Photon Sources

**Nanomaterials · Optoelectronics · Quantum confinement · Energy applications**

Academic report for **LMAPR2015 — Physics of Nanostructures**, UCLouvain (2026).

[**Read the full report (PDF)**](https://github.com/Lucasbog1010/Academic-Work/tree/main/Material_Engineering_Project/Nanostructure_Project/Nanostructure_Report.pdf)

## Overview

A collaborative technical study of quantum dots, explaining how nanoscale confinement changes semiconductor electronic and optical properties and examining their potential in photovoltaic devices and quantum photonics.

## Topics explored

- Quantum confinement and size-dependent band-gap tuning.
- Particle-in-a-box models and the relationship between dot dimensions and electronic energy levels.
- Optoelectronic behaviour and core/shell heterostructures (Type I, Type II and quasi-Type II).
- Colloidal quantum-dot production: LaMer nucleation/growth mechanism and hot-injection synthesis.
- Quantum-dot heterojunction and intermediate-band solar-cell concepts.
- Single-photon sources, optical selection rules, multiexciton effects, Auger recombination and blinking.
- Practical limitations, including nonradiative losses, stability and environmental considerations.
